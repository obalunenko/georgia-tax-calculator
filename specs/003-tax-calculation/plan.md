## Summary

Both interfaces collect a tax category, year income in GEL, and a list of
incomes, then call `service.Calculate`. The service parses the category,
converts each income to GEL through `service.Convert` (which uses the NBG
rate for the income date), calculates tax per income with `taxes.Calc`, and
sums the results with `pkg/moneyutils`.

## Technical Decisions

1. **`TaxType` enum with sentinel**: values between `taxTypeUnknown` and
   `taxTypeSentinel` are valid; display names come from line comments through
   stringer, and parsing uses the same names.
2. **Rate table**: `map[TaxType]TaxRate` with `float64` rates; lists are
   built from the map and sorted by the interfaces.
3. **Reuse conversion**: `Calculate` builds a `ConvertRequest` to GEL per
   income and calls `service.Convert`, so the same parsing and rate rules
   apply.
4. **Per-income rounding**: tax is rounded to 2 places for each income before
   summing.
5. **Year income**: entered by the user in GEL and reported as entered plus
   converted incomes; it does not affect the rate.

Decisions 4 and 5 describe the current code. The 2026-11-15 clarifications
in [spec.md](spec.md) change them (tax on the total, FR-004; limit warning,
FR-013; one calendar year, FR-014); ~~these changes are not implemented
